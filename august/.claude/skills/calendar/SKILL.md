# Calendar

Read and organize the **Outlook** calendar. Placing a personal hold is Tier 1; anything involving other people is Tier 2.

## Tiers

- **Reading the calendar** → **Tier 1.**
- **Placing a hold on Michael's own calendar with no external invitees** → **Tier 1.** Do it, report it.
- **Creating an event with invitees** → **Tier 2.** Draft it; wait for approval before sending invites.
- **Accepting or declining a meeting invitation** → **Tier 2.** Recommend a response; don't send it.

When a request is ambiguous about whether others are involved, assume Tier 2 and say so.

## Workflow

1. **Read the relevant window** in Outlook (today, the week, or the range Michael named).
2. **For "what's on my calendar":** list events in order with times, location, and a one-line prep note for anything that needs preparation (a hearing, a client meeting, a deposition).
3. **For a hold:** create it on Michael's calendar, no invitees, clearly titled (e.g., "HOLD — draft Garcia demand"). Report the time you took.
4. **For an event with people:** draft it fully — title, time, location, invitees, agenda/body — and present it. **Do not send invites** until Michael approves.
5. **For "when am I free":** propose concrete open slots, ranked, with your pick.

## Court-setting travel padding

When scheduling around a **court setting**, pad travel time. Fort Bend County courts (Richmond) and downtown Houston (Harris County) are a real drive apart, worse in traffic.

- Block travel **before and after** any in-person court appearance.
- Default pad: **60 minutes each way** for Fort Bend ↔ downtown Houston; **30 minutes** for local/same-area settings. Adjust for known traffic (morning dockets, rush hour) and flag if the pad collides with another commitment.
- Never schedule anything into the travel pad without flagging the conflict.

## Output

Bottom line first — the answer to what he asked (the day's schedule, the hold placed, the slots open) — then any conflicts or prep notes. For Tier 2 items, state clearly: "Drafted, not sent — approve to send invites."
