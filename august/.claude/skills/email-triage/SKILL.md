# Email Triage

Scan and organize the inbox. **Outlook is primary** (all firm business); **Gmail is triage-only** (personal — never send firm business from it). You **never send** — this skill produces buckets, flags, and draft replies.

## Workflow

1. **Scan both inboxes.** Outlook first (firm mail + anything time-sensitive), then Gmail. Pull unread and recent; note anything already flagged.

2. **Surface the priority senders at the top, regardless of bucket.** Before the buckets, list anything from:
   - **Courts** (clerks, coordinators, e-file notices)
   - **Opposing counsel**
   - **Adjusters / insurers**

   These get flagged at the very top with a one-line summary each, because a missed court or deadline email is the expensive kind.

3. **Bucket everything** into four:

   | Bucket | What goes here |
   |---|---|
   | **Needs Michael today** | Deadlines, court/opposing-counsel/adjuster items requiring action, anything time-critical |
   | **Needs response this week** | Client updates, provider follow-ups, non-urgent business needing a reply |
   | **FYI** | Confirmations, receipts, informational — no reply needed |
   | **Junk / unsubscribe candidates** | Marketing, cold outreach, list mail — flag for unsubscribe, don't act |

4. **Draft replies** for everything in the first two buckets. Firm business → draft in **Outlook** (reply draft, not sent). Plain-English register. Each draft ready for Michael to review, edit, and send.

## Tiers — read carefully

- **Reading, bucketing, labeling, archiving** email → **Tier 1.** Do it, report it.
- **Drafting replies** → **Tier 1** (drafts are inert). Draft freely for the top two buckets.
- **Sending any reply** → **Tier 2.** Never send. Present drafts; Michael sends.
- **Deleting / moving out of inbox** → *archive or label is Tier 1; **delete is Tier 2***. Don't delete anything without approval. Junk goes to a flagged list, not the trash.

## Output

Report to Michael, bottom line first:

```
Priority (courts / opposing counsel / adjusters): <n>
- <sender> — <one line> [draft queued]

Needs today: <n>   |   This week: <n>   |   FYI: <n>   |   Junk: <n>

## Needs Michael today
- <sender> — <one-line summary> — [reply drafted in Outlook]
...

## Needs response this week
- ...

## FYI
- ... (collapsed if long)

## Junk / unsubscribe candidates
- ... (flagged, not touched)
```

Keep FYI and Junk terse. Spend the detail where action is needed. Note which drafts are waiting in Outlook so Michael can review and send.
